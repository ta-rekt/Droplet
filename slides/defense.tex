\centering \huge Conclusions \\

\normalsize
\begin{itemize}
\item Optimization forces us to think about constraints \pause
\item Different constraints can give rise to qualitatively different behavior \pause
\item Evolution need not optimize individual components: sensing is a distributed process \pause
\item Averages don't tell the whole story: single microscopic events can be extremely informative
\end{itemize}
